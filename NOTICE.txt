Online play
-----------
Online play (System Link) goes through Steam using Spacewar (App ID 480),
Valve's public test app. There is no Tenchu Z server: players connect
directly to their Steam friends, and Steam shows them as playing "Spacewar".
It may stop working if Valve changes that app.
